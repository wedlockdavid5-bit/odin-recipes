Macaroni Cheese with Ham and Spring Onion

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 400g of macaroni pasta
  • 150g of soft processed cheese
  • 150ml of whipping cream
  • 1 spring onion, finely sliced
  • 4 slices of cooked ham, chopped
  • 1 handful of breadcrumbs
  • 25g of Parmesan, grated
  • salt to taste
  • pepper to taste

Method

  1. Place the pasta into a large pan of boiling salted water and cook until just soft. Drain well.
  2. Add the cheese and whipping cream into a pan over a low heat and bring to the boil, stirring well.
  3. Remove the sauce from the heat and beat well before adding the sliced spring onion and cooked ham, remove the pan from the heat.
  4. Add the pasta to the cheese sauce, season with salt and pepper.
  5. Sprinkle with breadcrumbs and the Parmesan, place the dish under a hot grill until cooked through. Serve the macaroni cheese immediately.
