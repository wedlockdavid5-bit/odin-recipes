Beef and Bacon Lasagne

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 200g beef mince
  • 200g lasagne pasta sheets
  • 50g streaky bacon
  • 100g onion, chopped
  • 50g carrots, chopped
  • 50g celery, chopped
  • 50g tomato purée
  • 800g tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 1 pinch cornflour, dissolved in 1 tsp water
  • 1 garlic clove, bruised
  • 1 handful fresh marjoram
  • 100g mushrooms, sliced
  • 250ml béchamel sauce
  • 25g cheddar, grated
  • Butter for greasing
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • Salt and black pepper to season
  • Water (for boiling pasta)

Method

  1. Gently heat the oil in a thick-bottomed pan, add the bacon, and fry for 2–3 minutes.
  2. Add the chopped onion, carrot, and celery. Cover with a lid and sweat for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the minced beef, increase the heat, and stir until lightly browned.
  4. Add the garlic, salt, pepper, and marjoram. Simmer gently for 15 minutes, then discard the bruised garlic clove.
  5. Remove from the heat and stir in the tomato purée, followed by the tinned tomatoes.
  6. Return to the heat and stir in the dissolved cornflour slurry.
  7. Mix in the mushrooms, bring back to a boil, cook for 2 minutes, then remove from the heat.
  8. Cook the lasagne sheets in boiling salted water according to pack instructions. Refresh in cold water and drain on a clean tea towel.
  9. Butter an ovenproof dish and coat the bottom with a layer of the meat sauce.
  10. Add a layer of lasagne pasta and cover with another layer of meat sauce.
  11. Add a final layer of pasta, top with the remaining meat sauce, and coat evenly with the béchamel sauce.
  12. Sprinkle with grated cheddar. Cover with foil or a lid and bake at 190°C (Gas Mark 5) for 20 minutes.
  13. Remove the lid and bake for a further 15 minutes until golden and bubbling. Serve hot.
